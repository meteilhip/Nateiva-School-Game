# Nateiva School Game 🦁🎮

> **Before making any changes, always read [SchoolGameSoul.md](./SchoolGameSoul.md).**

**Nateiva School Game** is a bilingual (French & English) interactive educational arcade and physics simulation suite designed for students from Pre-K (*Maternelle*) through Primary School (*SIL, CP, CE1, CE2, CM1, CM2*).

## 🔗 Official Repository & Production Deployment
- **GitHub Repository:** [https://github.com/meteilhip/Nateiva-School-Game](https://github.com/meteilhip/Nateiva-School-Game)
- **Vercel Project:** [https://vercel.com/nateiva-s-projects/nateiva-school-game](https://vercel.com/nateiva-s-projects/nateiva-school-game)
- **Live Production App:** [https://nateiva-school-game.vercel.app](https://nateiva-school-game.vercel.app)

## ✨ Key Modules & Features
- **📖 Lecture & Syllabes (Reading & Phonics):** Interactive syllable decomposition and word building.
- **🎙️ Prononciation (Pronunciation Studio):** Native French (`fr-FR-DeniseNeural`) and English (`en-US-JennyNeural`) studio voice modeling, slow-motion articulation, and acoustic feedback.
- **📚 Narration & Compréhension (Story Narration & Comprehension):** Bilingual audio stories with interactive comprehension challenges.
- **🧮 Mathématiques & Abaque (Interactive Math):** Tactile abacus, mental arithmetic, and visual word problems.
- **🧭 Labyrinthes & 📐 Puzzles Tangram:** Distinct procedural logic mazes and geometric tangram puzzles per activity.
- **🔬 Simulations Physiques Tactiles:** Direct finger/mouse drag-and-drop simulations (Chemistry Lab, Botany Germination, Archimedes' Buoyancy Tank, Electrical Circuit & Multimeter, Comet Defender Arcade).
