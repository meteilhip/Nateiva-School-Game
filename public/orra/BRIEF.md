# Orra by Nateiva — Product & Visual Specification (Restart from Scratch)

## Brand & Core Identity
- **Product Name:** Orra by Nateiva (`Orra`)
- **Tagline:** A Global Creator Platform — *Create. Engage. Sell. Grow.*
- **Core Value Proposition:** Build your own platform or website for events, voting, fundraising, products, community, and SMS campaigns — all in one place.
- **Palette Identity:** Obsidian Black (`#05070E`), Solar Gold (`#F59E0B` / `#FBBF24`), Ember Flame Red (`#EF4444` / `#DC2626`), Crisp Light Ink (`#F8FAFC`). ZERO violet / purple anywhere.

## 6 Core Creator Engines (Orbital Modules)
1. **🏆 Voting & Contests:** Pageants, awards, talent competitions with fraud-proof votes and instant Mobile Money / Card checkout.
2. **📅 Events & Ticketing:** Concerts, summits, festivals with QR passes and VIP tiers.
3. **💛 Fundraising & Causes:** Transparent impact campaigns, charity goals, diaspora crowdfunding.
4. **🛍️ Shop & Merchandise:** Branded creator apparel, tickets, physical goods, digital assets.
5. **👥 Community & Memberships:** Exclusive subscriber access, fan tiers, member directories.
6. **💬 SMS Campaign:** High-deliverability bulk SMS broadcasts, flash voting prompts, automated event reminders.

## Architecture & Visual System (Built with ScrollCraft)
- **Act 1 (Recognition · Hero):** Real-time Three.js 3D Earth Globe with realistic African continent lighting, golden city lights, gold-red dynamic orbital energy rings with particle trails, and 6 floating glassmorphism interactive cards that hover in 3D orbit around the globe.
- **Act 2 (Perspective · Architecture Blueprint):** Interactive structural view comparing fragmented disjointed tools against Orra's unified creator infrastructure.
- **Act 3 (The Turn · Signature Command Deck):** 3 Synchronized Real-time Perspectives:
  - Perspective 1: Super Admin Global Console (FCFA + Diaspora multi-currency analytics).
  - Perspective 2: Creator Back-office Workspace (African Stars Dashboard with live quick actions).
  - Perspective 3: Live Storefront & Pageant Voting Engine (Miss Africa 2025 interactive vote test).
- **Act 4 (Substance · Real-time Metrics):** Animated telemetry counters with FCFA & global payment settlement.
- **Act 5 (Range · 6-Engine Interactive Carousel):** In-depth module selector for all 6 engines.
- **Act 6 (Commitment · Workspace Initializer):** Interactive onboarding setup for creators.
