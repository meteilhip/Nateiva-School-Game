# Orra by Nateiva — Product & Visual Specification

## Brand & Core Identity
- **Product Name:** Orra by Nateiva (`Orra`)
- **Tagline:** A Global Creator Platform — *Create. Engage. Sell. Grow.*
- **Core Value Proposition:** Build your own platform or website for events, voting, fundraising, products, community, and SMS campaigns — all in one place.
- **Brand Palette:**
  - **Obsidian Black (`#05070E`, `#080B14`, `#0B0F19`, `#111827`):** Deep ground and elevated surfaces.
  - **Solar Gold (`#F59E0B`, `#FDE68A`):** Accents, badges, glowing borders, and icons.
  - **Red-Gold / Crimson (`#EF4444`, `#DC2626`):** Gradient shifts, primary call-to-actions, and atmospheric glows.
  - **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast headlines and body text for perfect legibility on all mobile screens.
  - **Zero violet / purple.**

## 90%+ Mobile-First Ergonomics
- **Touch-Friendly Controls:** 44px+ minimum hit targets for buttons, links, and chips.
- **Adaptive Canvas Stage:** 3D Africa globe with touch-action gestures (horizontal drags spin the globe; vertical swipes scroll naturally).
- **Adaptive Orbital System:** Radius and card dimensions scale smoothly from 360px budget Android phones up to 4K displays.
- **Fast 3G/4G Performance:** Lazy-loaded realistic assets, sub-second load times, lightweight hardware-accelerated transforms.

## 4-Step Creator Blueprint (`#how-it-works`)
1. **Step 01 · Identity & Domain:** Launch luxury website in 60s, custom domain (`yourname.com` or `yourname.orra.app`), SSL auto-provisioned.
2. **Step 02 · Modular Activities:** Add activities in one click (Voting, Events, Causes, Shop, Memberships, SMS) with interactive dynamic preview.
3. **Step 03 · Direct SMS Broadcast:** Reach 98% of fans directly on their phones across Africa (MTN, Orange, Moov, Airtel) and globally.
4. **Step 04 · Instant Payouts:** Real-time settlement to Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Orange Money) and international cards (Visa, Mastercard, Stripe).

## 6 Core Creator Engines (`#engines`)
1. **🏆 Voting & Contests:** Pageants, awards, talent competitions with fraud-proof votes and instant Mobile Money / Card checkout.
2. **📅 Events & Ticketing:** Concerts, summits, festivals with QR passes and VIP tiers.
3. **💛 Fundraising & Causes:** Transparent impact campaigns, charity goals, diaspora crowdfunding.
4. **🛍️ Shop & Merchandise:** Branded creator apparel, tickets, physical goods, digital assets.
5. **👥 Community & Memberships:** Exclusive subscriber access, fan tiers, member directories.
6. **💬 SMS Campaign:** High-deliverability bulk SMS broadcasts, flash voting prompts, automated event reminders.
