# Nateiva Arcade Design System

## Direction

An illustrated African field guide for playful discovery. The experience feels like opening a beautifully made storybook map: warm paper, forest greens, sunlight gold, and Nateiva red for important actions. Original clay story art gives each subject its own visual cue. The product stays lively and child-friendly while keeping its navigation and learning content easy to read.

## First Viewport

The home screen opens with Léo and Kiki in a Cameroonian savanna scene. Children choose their class and level, then launch a featured adventure or explore the subject library. The main image, short bilingual message, and visible play action establish the game before a child reads any instructions.

## Type

- Display: Fredoka, self-hosted
- Body and controls: Nunito, self-hosted
- Minimum body size: 16px where space allows; buttons remain large and legible.

## Interaction Grammar

- Discovery: explore a visual scene with touch, mouse, or keyboard.
- Arcade: tap or place visual tiles; math uses a matrix board with animated block choices.
- Mastery: solve a richer final challenge and see the learning action resolve on screen.
- Audio replay remains available beside task instructions. Preschool content stays audio-first.
- Correct answers use motion, visual progress, and sound; incorrect answers invite another try.
- Honor reduced-motion preferences and keep focus states visible.
